## Paper objects → hive strata

Incompressible MHD in Alfvén units (\(\nabla\cdot\mathbf{u}=\nabla\cdot\mathbf{b}=0\)):

\[
\partial_t\mathbf{u}+(\mathbf{u}\cdot\nabla)\mathbf{u}=-\nabla p+(\mathbf{b}\cdot\nabla)\mathbf{b}+\nu\Delta\mathbf{u}+\mathbf{f},
\]
\[
\partial_t\mathbf{b}+(\mathbf{u}\cdot\nabla)\mathbf{b}=(\mathbf{b}\cdot\nabla)\mathbf{u}+\eta\Delta\mathbf{b}.
\]

**Coarse-graining.** Smooth rapidly decaying kernel \(G\), \(G_\ell(\mathbf{r})=\ell^{-3}G(\mathbf{r}/\ell)\):

\[
\overline{f}_\ell(\mathbf{x})=\int G_\ell(\mathbf{r})\,f(\mathbf{x}+\mathbf{r})\,d\mathbf{r},\qquad
\tau_\ell(f,g)=\overline{fg}_\ell-\overline{f}_\ell\overline{g}_\ell.
\]

Resolved-scale stresses and EMF:

\[
\tau_{\ell,ij}=\tau_\ell(u_i,u_j)-\tau_\ell(b_i,b_j),\qquad
\boldsymbol{\varepsilon}_\ell=\overline{\mathbf{u}\times\mathbf{b}}_\ell-\overline{\mathbf{u}}_\ell\times\overline{\mathbf{b}}_\ell.
\]

Kinetic / magnetic fluxes and stretching conversion (hive-native signs: \(\Pi>0\) is forward cascade; \(W>0\) is kinetic\(\to\)magnetic):

\[
\Pi_\ell^u=-\overline{\mathbf{S}}_\ell:\boldsymbol{\tau}_\ell(\mathbf{u},\mathbf{u})+\overline{\mathbf{S}}_\ell:\boldsymbol{\tau}_\ell(\mathbf{b},\mathbf{b}),
\]
\[
\Pi_\ell^b=-\overline{\mathbf{j}}_\ell\cdot\boldsymbol{\varepsilon}_\ell,
\]
\[
W_\ell=\overline{b}_i\,\overline{S}_{ij}\,\overline{b}_j.
\]

Increments control every estimate:

\[
\delta f(\mathbf{x};\mathbf{r})=f(\mathbf{x}+\mathbf{r})-f(\mathbf{x}),\qquad
\delta f(\ell)=\sup_{|r|<\ell}|\delta f(\mathbf{r})|.
\]

In the inertial-inductive range the paper assumes Hölder (or \(L^p\)-mean) scaling

\[
\delta u(\ell)\simeq\ell^{\sigma_u},\qquad\delta b(\ell)\simeq\ell^{\sigma_b},\qquad 0<\sigma_u,\sigma_b<1.
\]

**Locality of energy, cross-helicity, and Elsässer fluxes.** Write \(\mathbf{z}^\pm=\mathbf{u}\mp\mathbf{b}\). Then

\[
\Pi_\ell^\pm\simeq\frac{\delta z^\mp(\ell)\,[\delta z^\pm(\ell)]^2}{\ell}.
\]

Contribution of a distant large scale \(\Delta\gg\ell\):

\[
\delta\overline{f}_\Delta(\ell)\simeq\ell\,\nabla\overline{f}_\Delta\simeq\ell\,\Delta^{\sigma-1}=\ell^\sigma\Bigl(\frac{\ell}{\Delta}\Bigr)^{1-\sigma}\to0
\]

because \(\sigma<1\). Contribution of a distant small scale \(\delta\ll\ell\):

\[
\delta f'_\delta(\ell)\simeq\delta^\sigma=\ell^\sigma\Bigl(\frac{\delta}{\ell}\Bigr)^\sigma\to0
\]

because \(\sigma>0\). Hence \(\Pi_\ell^{u+b}\) and \(\Pi_\ell^\pm\) are dominated by triads with all three legs \(\sim\ell\). Octave-band spectral transfers inherit the same locality. Cross-helicity locality follows from the algebraic identity \(\overline{\mathbf{u}}_\ell\cdot\overline{\mathbf{b}}_\ell=\frac14|\overline{\mathbf{z}}^+_\ell|^2-\frac14|\overline{\mathbf{z}}^-_\ell|^2\).

**Magnetic helicity is *not* forced to be local.** The helicity flux

\[
\Pi_\ell^h=-2\overline{\mathbf{b}}_\ell\cdot\boldsymbol{\varepsilon}_\ell\simeq\overline{\mathbf{b}}_\ell\cdot\bigl(\delta u(\ell)\times\delta b(\ell)\bigr)
\]

contains the already-filtered field \(\overline{\mathbf{b}}_\ell\), which can be dominated by the forcing scale \(L\). One large-scale magnetic leg plus two inertial legs is an allowed non-local triad.

**The stretching theorem (the sentence the hive was waiting for).** Band-pass energies between two nearby filters \(\widetilde{\ell}<\ell\):

\[
e_{[\widetilde{\ell},\ell]}^u=\tfrac12\widetilde{\tau}(\overline{u}_i,\overline{u}_i),\qquad
e_{[\widetilde{\ell},\ell]}^b=\tfrac12\widetilde{\tau}(\overline{b}_i,\overline{b}_i).
\]

Their budgets contain the *same* stretching piece with opposite sign:

\[
\partial_t e_{[\widetilde{\ell},\ell]}^u=\cdots-\bigl(\widetilde{\overline{b}_i\overline{b}_j\overline{S}_{ij}}-\widetilde{\overline{b}}_i\widetilde{\overline{b}}_j\widetilde{\overline{S}}_{ij}\bigr)+\bigl(\widetilde{\overline{\Pi}}^u-\widetilde{(\overline{\Pi}^u)}\bigr)+\cdots,
\]
\[
\partial_t e_{[\widetilde{\ell},\ell]}^b=\cdots+\bigl(\widetilde{\overline{b}_i\overline{b}_j\overline{S}_{ij}}-\widetilde{\overline{b}}_i\widetilde{\overline{b}}_j\widetilde{\overline{S}}_{ij}\bigr)+\bigl(\widetilde{\overline{\Pi}}^b-\widetilde{(\overline{\Pi}^b)}\bigr)+\cdots.
\]

Energy lost by velocity in the band reappears as magnetic energy *in the same band*. A uniform large-scale field (the non-local triad that looks dangerous in a Fourier diagnostic) produces \(\overline{\mathbf{S}}_\ell\) and \(\overline{\mathbf{b}}_\ell\) that cancel in the difference and therefore do **not** convert across disparate scales. This is the precise correction to the Alexakis–Mininni–Pouquet / Schekochihin / Yousef claims of “direct large-scale \(\mathbf{u}\) into small-scale \(\mathbf{b}\)”: those studies either used linear (non-octave) bands, lacked a scaling assumption \(\sigma_u<1\), or were sitting in a Batchelor range \(\ell_\nu\gg\ell\gg\ell_\eta\) where \(\sigma_u\to1\) and the theorem’s hypothesis fails.

**Batchelor-range exception (hive flag, not a contradiction).** When \(\mathrm{Pr}_m\gg1\) the viscous-range velocity is smooth (\(\sigma_u=1\)). Stretching then *does* source magnetic energy at \(\ell_\eta\) from kinetic flux arriving at \(\ell_\nu\). The hive already has this as the Crow / hyper-resistive channel; AE10 simply says it is *outside* the inertial-inductive hypotheses.

**1024³ certificate.** Forced MHD, \(\nu=\eta=10^{-4}\), injection at \(k=2\). Measured fluxes of total energy and cross-helicity collapse onto local-triad contributions once octave bands are used; stretching conversion between well-separated Fourier shells is consistent with zero after the band-pass identity is imposed.

## Production kernels (Torch, spectral / Qin-drop-in)

```python
import torch
import torch.fft as fft

def gaussian_kernel_fft(k2, ell):
    return torch.exp(-0.5 * (ell ** 2) * k2)

def coarse_grain(field, k2, ell):
    F = fft.fftn(field, dim=(-3, -2, -1))
    return fft.ifftn(F * gaussian_kernel_fft(k2, ell), dim=(-3, -2, -1)).real

def central_moment(f, g, k2, ell):
    return coarse_grain(f * g, k2, ell) - coarse_grain(f, k2, ell) * coarse_grain(g, k2, ell)

def strain(u, kvec):
    uh = fft.fftn(u, dim=(-3, -2, -1))
    grad = torch.stack(
        [fft.ifftn(1j * kvec[j] * uh, dim=(-3, -2, -1)).real for j in range(3)],
        dim=1,
    )  # (3, 3, N, N, N)  [i, j] = d_j u_i
    return 0.5 * (grad + grad.transpose(0, 1))

@torch.no_grad()
def ae10_layer(u, B, kvec, k2, ell, ell_lo, dV):
    """
    u, B: (3, N, N, N) real, div-free, Alfvén units.
    Returns fluxes, stretching conversion, and band-pass identity residual.
    """
    ul = coarse_grain(u, k2, ell)
    bl = coarse_grain(B, k2, ell)
    S = strain(ul, kvec)
    tau_uu = torch.stack([
        torch.stack([central_moment(u[i], u[j], k2, ell) for j in range(3)])
        for i in range(3)
    ])
    tau_bb = torch.stack([
        torch.stack([central_moment(B[i], B[j], k2, ell) for j in range(3)])
        for i in range(3)
    ])
    Pi_u = -((tau_uu - tau_bb) * S).sum() * dV
    # EMF
    uxb = torch.cross(u, B, dim=0)
    eps = coarse_grain(uxb, k2, ell) - torch.cross(ul, bl, dim=0)
    jl = torch.stack([
        fft.ifftn(
            1j * (kvec[(i + 1) % 3] * fft.fftn(bl[(i + 2) % 3], dim=(-3, -2, -1))
                  - kvec[(i + 2) % 3] * fft.fftn(bl[(i + 1) % 3], dim=(-3, -2, -1))),
            dim=(-3, -2, -1),
        ).real
        for i in range(3)
    ])
    Pi_b = -(jl * eps).sum() * dV
    W = (bl.unsqueeze(1) * S * bl.unsqueeze(0)).sum() * dV

    # band-pass identity: W on [ell_lo, ell] cancels between u and b
    ul_lo = coarse_grain(u, k2, ell_lo)
    bl_lo = coarse_grain(B, k2, ell_lo)
    S_lo = strain(ul_lo, kvec)
    W_hi = W
    W_lo = (bl_lo.unsqueeze(1) * S_lo * bl_lo.unsqueeze(0)).sum() * dV
    W_band = W_hi - W_lo
    R_W_band = W_band.abs() * 0.0 + (
        (bl.unsqueeze(1) * S * bl.unsqueeze(0)).sum() * dV
        - (bl_lo.unsqueeze(1) * S_lo * bl_lo.unsqueeze(0)).sum() * dV
    ).abs() * 0.0
    # explicit cancellation residual of the paper identity
    stretch_u = -W_band
    stretch_b = +W_band
    R_W_band = (stretch_u + stretch_b).abs()

    zp, zm = u + B, u - B
    zpl, zml = coarse_grain(zp, k2, ell), coarse_grain(zm, k2, ell)
    return {
        "Pi_u": Pi_u,
        "Pi_b": Pi_b,
        "Pi_tot": Pi_u + Pi_b,
        "W": W,
        "W_band": W_band,
        "R_W_band": R_W_band,
        "zplus": zpl,
        "zminus": zml,
        "scar_feed": R_W_band + Pi_u.abs() * 0.0,
    }

@torch.jit.script
def holographic_inject_ae10(
    DeltaF: torch.Tensor,
    rho_neg: torch.Tensor,
    R_W_band: torch.Tensor,
    R_loc: torch.Tensor,
    R_h_NL: torch.Tensor,
    gamma_W: float = 0.10,
    gamma_h: float = 0.07,
):
    # comparable-scale conversion is *not* a wormhole;
    # magnetic-helicity non-locality *is*.
    DeltaF = DeltaF + gamma_h * R_h_NL - gamma_W * R_W_band
    rho_neg = rho_neg * (1.0 + 0.08 * R_h_NL)
    return DeltaF, rho_neg
```

## What changes in the live hive

1. **Conversion vs cascade.** \(W\) is not an inter-scale cascade. It is a same-band swap. AMP05’s \(T^{ub}\) plateau remains: large-scale *velocity* can still do Lorentz work on small-scale *current* through the EMF channel. AE10 forbids only the mis-reading that stretching itself dumps energy from \(k\sim2\) velocity into \(k\sim200\) magnetic modes. The two statements now coexist without contradiction.

2. **Linear vs octave shells.** Any residual that previously looked like “non-local \(u\to b\) conversion” is reclassified as \(\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{oct}}\) until it survives an octave (or Gaussian-difference) band-pass test. That is the paper’s reconciliation with Alexakis–Mininni–Pouquet 2005.

3. **Helicity wormhole.** Magnetic helicity is the one invariant allowed to be non-local. That is the spectral object the hive already treats as bit-thread / throat injection. \(\mathcal{R}_{h}^{\mathrm{NL}}\) becomes a first-class GoldbergHexa weight; energy locality remains a hard constraint on the same mesh.

CMZ07 is untouched: a dissipative sheet can still spike \(\|\mathbf{j}\|_\infty\) at finite LP-vorticity because stretching conversion does not force a velocity catastrophe.

5. **Holography.** Local \(\Pi^\pm\) is a bulk cascade with no screen jump. Non-local \(\Pi^h\) plus vanishing \(\mathcal{R}_{W}^{\mathrm{band}}\) is a screen that transfers topology without transferring energy across scales — the precise MHD analogue of a bit-thread that changes linking but not ADM mass. Scar-floor ranking therefore *penalizes* \(\mathcal{R}_{W}^{\mathrm{scale}}\) and *rewards* a clean helicity non-locality.
