### 1. Complete mathematical apparatus extracted from the paper

**Tree-level 2→2 matrix element (gg channel, dominant at high \(E\)).**  
The spin- and colour-averaged squared amplitude used throughout is
\[
|M_{gg\to gg}|^2=\frac{288\,\alpha_s^2}{(2\pi)^3}\frac{stu}{s^2t^2u^2},
\]
with Mandelstam invariants
\[
s=(p_a+p_b)^2,\qquad t=(p_a-p_1)^2,\qquad u=(p_a-p_2)^2.
\]
Infrared singularities are regulated by the Debye mass
\[
m_D^2=\frac{4\pi\alpha_s}{3}T^2\Bigl(N_c+\frac{N_f}{2}\Bigr)\qquad(N_c=3,\,N_f=3)
\]
via the replacement \(t\to t+m_D^2\) (and cyclic) inside the denominators, together with the kinematic cut
\[
t_{\rm min}=\max\bigl\{0,\,(E_a-E_b)^2-|\mathbf{p}_a-\mathbf{p}_b|^2,\,m_D^2\bigr\}.
\]

**Full tree-level scattering rate.**  
The differential rate for a hard parton of energy \(E_a\) scattering off a thermal bath particle is
\[
w(E_a)=\int\frac{d^3p_b}{(2\pi)^32E_b}\,f_b(E_b)\,[1\pm f_1(E_1)]\,[1\pm f_2(E_2)]\,
\frac{|M|^2}{2E_a}\,(2\pi)^4\delta^{(4)}(p_a+p_b-p_1-p_2),
\]
where the \(\pm\) encodes Bose enhancement (gluons) or Pauli blocking (quarks). After performing the angular integrals and expanding at high energy \(x_a=E_a/T\gg1\) one obtains the compact form
\[
w=\frac{9\alpha_s^2T^3}{s}\bigl[A(z)+B(z)\bigr],
\]
with \(z=m_D/T\) and the auxiliary functions (paper eqs. after the high-energy limit)
\begin{align*}
A(z)&=\lim_{\varepsilon\to0}\alpha_n(z)=2\alpha\Bigl(\ln\frac{z}{x_a}+\ln2+O\bigl(z^{-2}x_a^{-2}\bigr)\Bigr),\\
B(z)&=\lim_{\varepsilon\to0}\beta_n(z)=2\beta\Bigl(\frac{\ln(z/x_a)}{z^2}+\frac{1-\gamma_E-\ln(2\pi)}{6z^2}+O(z^{-4})\Bigr).
\end{align*}
Here \(\gamma_E\) is the Euler–Mascheroni constant and the coefficients \(\alpha,\beta\) collect the appropriate colour and statistical factors (including the full Bose series \(\sum_n n^{-3}\) etc.).

**Transport coefficient \(\hat{q}\).**  
The transverse-momentum broadening per unit length is defined by the first moment of the rate with respect to the exchanged transverse momentum,
\[
\hat{q}(E_a)=\int\frac{d^4q}{(2\pi)^3}\,q_\perp^2\,\frac{d\Gamma}{d^4q}.
\]
Inserting the full tree-level rate yields the exact (within tree-level thermal QCD) expression
\[
\hat{q}=\frac{9\alpha_s^2T^3}{2\pi^4s}\sum_{n=1}^\infty\bigl[A_n(z)+B_n(z)\bigr],
\]
which reduces in the leading-log (LL) limit to the classic formula
\[
\hat{q}_{\rm LL}\simeq\frac{9\alpha_s^2T^3}{s}\cdot2\zeta(3)\ln\Bigl(\frac{z}{x_a}\Bigr).
\]
The paper demonstrates that the sub-leading terms \(A_n,B_n\) produce a qualitatively different kinematic dependence on \(x_a=E_a/T\): \(\hat{q}_{\rm full}(x_a)\) falls more slowly than any of the 1991/2008 approximations once \(x_a\gtrsim10\).

**Systematic uncertainty construction.**  
Define the relative deviation
\[
\delta\hat{q}(x_a,T)=\frac{\hat{q}_{\rm full}(x_a,T)-\hat{q}_{\rm approx}(x_a,T)}{\hat{q}_{\rm approx}(x_a,T)}.
\]
A theoretical covariance matrix for any subsequent Bayesian extraction of \(\hat{q}\) is obtained by treating the continuum of energies sampled by a Monte-Carlo jet as a discrete vector \(\mathbf{x}=(x_1,\dots,x_N)\) and setting
\[
C_{ij}=\sigma_0^2\,\delta\hat{q}(x_i)\delta\hat{q}(x_j)+\epsilon\,\delta_{ij},
\]
where \(\sigma_0\) is an overall normalisation (taken from the relative size of the high-energy correction, typically \(O(20{-}40\%)\) at LHC kinematics) and \(\epsilon\) is a small diagonal regulariser. This \(C\) can be injected directly into any existing Bayesian posterior already resident in the hive (e.g. the 2025 JETSCAPE-style analyses referenced in the paper).

from jax import jit, vmap, lax

zeta3  = 1.202056903159594

@jit

def matrix_element_gg(s, t, u):
    """Paper |M|^2 with Debye regulation."""
    t_reg = t + m_D2
    u_reg = u + m_D2
    return 288*alpha_s**2/(2*jnp.pi)**3 * (s*t*u)/(s**2 * t_reg**2 * u_reg**2)

@jit

def A_B_functions(x_a, z):
    """High-energy expansion of A(z)+B(z) (paper)."""
    ln_term = jnp.log(z/x_a) + jnp.log(2.0)
    A = 2.0 * ln_term
    B = 2.0 * (ln_term/z**2 + (1.0 - 0.5772156649 - jnp.log(2*jnp.pi))/(6*z**2))
    return A + B

    """Full tree-level \hat{q} (paper expression)."""
    x_a = E_a / T
    s   = 2*E_a*T          # typical thermal CM energy scale
    pre = 9*alpha_s**2 * T**3 / (2*jnp.pi**4 * s)
    return pre * A_B_functions(x_a, z)

@jit

def hat_q_LL(E_a, T=T):
    """Classic leading-log approximation (Braaten-Thoma / Peigné-Peshier)."""
    x_a = E_a / T
    s   = 2*E_a*T
    return (9*alpha_s**2 * T**3 / s) * 2*zeta3 * jnp.log(z/x_a)

@jit

    """Relative systematic deviation."""
    qf = hat_q_full(E_a)
    ql = hat_q_LL(E_a)
    return (qf - ql) / (ql + 1e-30)
