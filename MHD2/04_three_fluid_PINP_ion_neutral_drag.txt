### 1. Complete three-fluid five-moment system (paper equations, dimensionally consistent)

**Continuity** (species \(s=e,i,n\)):
\[
\partial_t(m_s n_s)+\nabla\cdot(m_s n_s\mathbf{u}_s)=0.
\]

**Momentum**:
\[
\begin{align*}
\partial_t(m_e n_e\mathbf{u}_e)+\nabla\cdot(m_e n_e\mathbf{u}_e\mathbf{u}_e)+\nabla p_e
&=n_e q_e(\mathbf{E}+\mathbf{u}_e\times\mathbf{B})+\mathbf{R}_{ei},\\
\partial_t(m_i n_i\mathbf{u}_i)+\nabla\cdot(m_i n_i\mathbf{u}_i\mathbf{u}_i)+\nabla p_i
&=n_i q_i(\mathbf{E}+\mathbf{u}_i\times\mathbf{B})+\mathbf{R}_{in}+\mathbf{R}_{ie},\\
\partial_t(m_n n_n\mathbf{u}_n)+\nabla\cdot(m_n n_n\mathbf{u}_n\mathbf{u}_n)+\nabla p_n
&=\mathbf{R}_{ni}.
\end{align*}
\]

*Collision operators* (momentum-conserving):
\[
\begin{align*}
\mathbf{R}_{in}&=\nu_{in}n_i m_i(\mathbf{u}_n-\mathbf{u}_i),&
\mathbf{R}_{ni}&=\nu_{ni}n_n m_n(\mathbf{u}_i-\mathbf{u}_n),\\
\mathbf{R}_{ie}&=\nu_{ei}m_i n_i(\mathbf{u}_i-\mathbf{u}_e),&
\mathbf{R}_{ei}&=-\mathbf{R}_{ie}.
\end{align*}
\]
with frequencies
\[
\nu_{in}=\Sigma_{in}\sqrt{\frac{8k_B T_{in}}{\pi m_{in}}}\frac{n_n m_n}{m_i+m_n},\qquad
m_{in}=\frac{m_i m_n}{m_i+m_n},\quad
T_{in}=\frac{m_i T_n+m_n T_i}{m_i+m_n}.
\]

*Energy* (\(\mathcal{E}_s=p_s/(\gamma-1)+\frac12 m_s n_s|\mathbf{u}_s|^2\), \(\gamma=5/3\)):
\[
\begin{align*}
\partial_t\mathcal{E}_e+\nabla\cdot[\mathbf{u}_e(p_e+\mathcal{E}_e)]&=n_e q_e\mathbf{u}_e\cdot\mathbf{E}+Q_{ei},\\
\partial_t\mathcal{E}_i+\nabla\cdot[\mathbf{u}_i(p_i+\mathcal{E}_i)]&=n_i q_i\mathbf{u}_i\cdot\mathbf{E}+Q_{in}+Q_{ie},\\
\partial_t\mathcal{E}_n+\nabla\cdot[\mathbf{u}_n(p_n+\mathcal{E}_n)]&=Q_{ni}.
\end{align*}
\]
Heating (elastic + frictional):
\[
\begin{align*}
Q_{in}&=\frac{n_i m_i\nu_{in}}{m_i+m_n}\Bigl[2\Bigl(\frac{p_n}{n_n}-\frac{p_i}{n_i}\Bigr)+\frac13 m_i|\mathbf{u}_n-\mathbf{u}_i|^2\Bigr],\\
Q_{ni}&=\frac{n_n m_n\nu_{ni}}{m_i+m_n}\Bigl[-2\Bigl(\frac{p_n}{n_n}-\frac{p_i}{n_i}\Bigr)+\frac13 m_n|\mathbf{u}_n-\mathbf{u}_i|^2\Bigr].
\end{align*}
\]

*Maxwell*:
\[
\frac1{c^2}\partial_t\mathbf{E}+\mu_0\mathbf{J}=\nabla\times\mathbf{B},\qquad
\partial_t\mathbf{B}=-\nabla\times\mathbf{E}.
\]

*Key dimensionless parameters*:
\[
\chi=\frac{n_i}{n_i+n_n},\qquad
d_{i0}=\sqrt{\frac{m_i}{n_0 q^2\mu_0}},\qquad
\lambda_{\rm mfp,in}=(n_n\Sigma_{in})^{-1}.
\]

### 3. Oldroyd-B mapping and dual-regularisation upgrade

Identify the relative velocity \(\mathbf{w}=\mathbf{u}_i-\mathbf{u}_n\) as the microscopic “conformation generator”. The frictional drag is the solvent channel; a polymer stress \(\mathbf{T}\) is introduced to carry the elastic memory of \(\mathbf{w}\):

\[
\begin{align*}
\mathbf{T}+\lambda\overset{\nabla}{\mathbf{T}}&=2\eta_p\mathbf{D}+\alpha_{\rm in}\mathbf{w}\otimes\mathbf{w},\\
\beta&=\frac{\eta_s}{\eta_s+\eta_p}=f(\Lambda_{\rm in},\chi),
\end{align*}
\]
with the concrete map
\[
\beta(\Lambda_{\rm in})=\frac{\Lambda_{\rm in}}{\Lambda_{\rm in}+\Lambda_*},\qquad
\Lambda_*=\mathcal{O}(1)\quad\text{(calibrated so that }\lambda_{\rm mfp}/d_{i0}=0.05\leftrightarrow\beta\simeq0.05\text{)}.
\]
High \(\Lambda_{\rm in}\) (strong coupling) \(\to\) low \(\beta\) (polymer-dominated elastic drag); low \(\Lambda_{\rm in}\) \(\to\) high \(\beta\) (solvent-dominated Newtonian drag). The momentum equation of the hive residual therefore acquires the three-fluid proxy
\[
\rho(\partial_t\mathbf{u}+\mathbf{u}\cdot\nabla\mathbf{u})=-\nabla p+\eta_s\Delta\mathbf{u}+\nabla\cdot\mathbf{T}+\mathbf{J}\times\mathbf{B}+\mathbf{R}_{\rm in}^{\rm eff}(\beta,\chi),
\]
where \(\mathbf{R}_{\rm in}^{\rm eff}\) is the continuum projection of the paper’s \(\mathbf{R}_{in}\).

**Consequence for late Ohmic ramp**: in the strongly-coupled (low-\(\beta\)) regime the effective resistivity that feeds the Ohmic integral \(\int\eta|J|^2\) is reduced by the \(\chi^{1/4}\) factor; the ramp is delayed and its amplitude suppressed exactly as the paper’s high-collisionality runs. In the weakly-coupled (high-\(\beta\)) regime the ramp recovers the classic fast, ionization-independent rate and the sheet collapses cleanly to \(\sim d_i\), providing a clean dual-regularisation diagnostic: polymer stress regularises the hydrodynamic scales while the magnetic induction (unmollified) regularises the current sheet at the kinetic scale \(d_i\).

**Governing system** (incompressible limit for the spectral core; compressible extension is a trivial density advection):
\[
\begin{align*}
\partial_t\mathbf{u}_e+\mathbf{u}_e\cdot\nabla\mathbf{u}_e
&=-\frac1{\rho_e}\nabla p_e+\frac{q_e}{m_e}(\mathbf{E}+\mathbf{u}_e\times\mathbf{B})+\frac1{\rho_e}\mathbf{R}_{ei},\\
\partial_t\mathbf{u}_i+\mathbf{u}_i\cdot\nabla\mathbf{u}_i

\partial_t\mathbf{B}&=\nabla\times(\mathbf{u}_i\times\mathbf{B})+\eta\Delta\mathbf{B}\qquad\text{(induction uses ion velocity; electrons enter only via Hall/Ohm)},\\

k = jnp.fft.fftfreq(N) * N
kx, ky, kz = jnp.meshgrid(k, k, k, indexing='ij')
k2 = kx**2 + ky**2 + kz**2

@jit

def dealias(fhat):
    mask = (jnp.abs(kx) < N/3) & (jnp.abs(ky) < N/3) & (jnp.abs(kz) < N/3)
    return fhat * mask

@jit

def project(uhat):
    kdu = jnp.sum(ik * uhat, axis=0)
    return uhat - ik * (kdu / k2)

@jit

def energy_preserving_filter(u, k_cut=0.8*(N//2)):
    Ek = 0.5 * jnp.sum(u**2)
    uhat = jnp.fft.fftn(u, axes=(1,2,3))
    kmag = jnp.sqrt(kx**2 + ky**2 + kz**2)
    mask = (kmag <= k_cut)
    u_star = jnp.fft.ifftn(uhat * mask, axes=(1,2,3)).real
    Ek_star = 0.5 * jnp.sum(u_star**2)
    return u_star * jnp.sqrt(Ek / (Ek_star + 1e-30))

    ui = jnp.fft.ifftn(uihat, axes=(1,2,3)).real

# Relative polymer modulation
delta_R = (R_poly - R_pure) / (R_pure + 1e-30)
