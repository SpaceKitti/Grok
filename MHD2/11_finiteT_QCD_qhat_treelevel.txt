### 1. Complete mathematical apparatus extracted from the paper

**Continuous incompressible MHD (non-dimensional vorticity form)**  
On a bounded contractible Lipschitz domain \(\Omega\subset\mathbb{R}^d\) (\(d=2,3\)) with space-time cylinder \(\Omega\times(0,T]\):

\[
\begin{aligned}
\partial_t\mathbf{u}+\boldsymbol{\omega}\times\mathbf{u}+R_f^{-1}\nabla\times\boldsymbol{\omega}-\mathsf{c}(\nabla\times\mathbf{H})\times\mathbf{H}+\nabla P&=\mathbf{f},\\
\boldsymbol{\omega}-\nabla\times\mathbf{u}&=\mathbf{0},\qquad\nabla\cdot\mathbf{u}=0,\\
\partial_t\mathbf{H}+R_m^{-1}\nabla\times(\nabla\times\mathbf{H})-\nabla\times(\mathbf{u}\times\mathbf{H})&=\mathbf{0},
\end{aligned}
\]

where \(\boldsymbol{\omega}=\nabla\times\mathbf{u}\) is vorticity, \(P=p+\frac12|\mathbf{u}|^2\) is total pressure, \(R_f\) is the fluid Reynolds number, \(R_m\) the magnetic Reynolds number, and \(\mathsf{c}\) the coupling number. The system is supplemented with general (mixed) boundary conditions:

- Fluid: \(P=\widehat{P}\) on \(\Gamma_{\widehat{P}}\), \(\mathbf{u}\cdot\mathbf{n}=\hat{u}\) on \(\Gamma_{\hat{u}}\), \(\mathbf{u}\times\mathbf{n}=\widehat{\mathbf{u}}\) on \(\Gamma_{\widehat{\mathbf{u}}}\), \(\mathbf{n}\times(\boldsymbol{\omega}\times\mathbf{n})=\widehat{\boldsymbol{\omega}}\) on \(\Gamma_{\widehat{\boldsymbol{\omega}}}\).
- Maxwell: \(\mathbf{n}\times\mathbf{E}=\widehat{\mathbf{E}}\) on \(\Gamma_{\widehat{\mathbf{E}}}\) (and compatible tangential/normal conditions on the complementary partitions).

Initial data: \(\mathbf{u}(\cdot,0)=\mathbf{u}^0\) (divergence-free), \(\mathbf{H}(\cdot,0)=\mathbf{H}^0\) (divergence-free).

**Function spaces and discrete de Rham complex**  
The infinite-dimensional de Rham sequence

\[
H^1(\Omega)\;\hookrightarrow\;H(\mathrm{curl};\Omega)\;\hookrightarrow\;H(\mathrm{div};\Omega)\;\hookrightarrow\;L^2(\Omega)
\]

is discretized by a commuting diagram of finite-element spaces

\[
\mathcal{G}_h(\Omega)\;\hookrightarrow\;\mathcal{C}_h(\Omega)\;\hookrightarrow\;\mathcal{D}_h(\Omega)\;\hookrightarrow\;\mathcal{S}_h(\Omega)\to0
\]

(typically continuous Lagrange for scalars, Nédélec edge elements for \(\mathcal{C}_h\), Raviart–Thomas face elements for \(\mathcal{D}_h\)). Exact sequence properties guarantee that discrete gradients, curls and divergences preserve the kernel relations \(\nabla\times\nabla=0\), \(\nabla\cdot\nabla\times=0\).

**Semi-discrete structure-preserving weak form**  
Seek \((\mathbf{u}_h,\boldsymbol{\omega}_h,P_h,\mathbf{H}_h)\) in the appropriate discrete spaces such that, for all admissible test functions \(\mathbf{v}_h,\mathbf{w}_h,q_h,\mathbf{b}_h\),

\[
\begin{aligned}
\langle\partial_t\mathbf{u}_h,\mathbf{v}_h\rangle+a(\boldsymbol{\omega}_h,\mathbf{u}_h,\mathbf{v}_h)+R_f^{-1}\langle\nabla\times\boldsymbol{\omega}_h,\mathbf{v}_h\rangle\\
-\mathsf{c}\,a(\nabla\times\mathbf{H}_h,\mathbf{H}_h,\mathbf{v}_h)-\langle P_h,\nabla\cdot\mathbf{v}_h\rangle&=\langle\mathbf{f},\mathbf{v}_h\rangle-\text{boundary terms},\\
-\langle\mathbf{u}_h,\nabla\times\mathbf{w}_h\rangle+\langle\boldsymbol{\omega}_h,\mathbf{w}_h\rangle&=-\text{boundary terms},\\
\langle\nabla\cdot\mathbf{u}_h,q_h\rangle&=0,\\
\langle\partial_t\mathbf{H}_h,\mathbf{b}_h\rangle+R_m^{-1}\langle\nabla\times\mathbf{H}_h,\nabla\times\mathbf{b}_h\rangle-a(\mathbf{u}_h,\mathbf{H}_h,\nabla\times\mathbf{b}_h)&=\text{boundary terms}.
\end{aligned}
\]

The trilinear form

\[
a(\boldsymbol{\alpha},\boldsymbol{\beta},\boldsymbol{\gamma})=\langle\boldsymbol{\alpha}\times\boldsymbol{\beta},\boldsymbol{\gamma}\rangle_\Omega
\]

is skew-symmetric. Consequently the formulation conserves mass *strongly* (\(\nabla\cdot\mathbf{u}_h=0\) pointwise in the discrete sense), conserves charge *strongly*, enforces Gauss’s law for magnetism *weakly*, and dissipates energy at the exact physical rate (viscous + ohmic).

**Leapfrog-type temporal decoupling (staggered mid-point)**  
Let \(\Delta t>0\) and introduce the staggered sequences \(\{t_k=k\Delta t\}\) and \(\{t_{k+1/2}\}\). The fully discrete scheme proceeds as follows:

1. **Fluid step** (at integer times, using magnetic field frozen from the previous half-step):
   \[
   \begin{aligned}
   &\Bigl\langle\frac{\mathbf{u}_h^{k+1}-\mathbf{u}_h^k}{\Delta t},\mathbf{v}_h\Bigr\rangle
   +a\bigl(\boldsymbol{\omega}_h^{k+1/2},\mathbf{u}_h^{k+1/2},\mathbf{v}_h\bigr)
   +R_f^{-1}\langle\nabla\times\boldsymbol{\omega}_h^{k+1/2},\mathbf{v}_h\rangle\\
   &\qquad-\mathsf{c}\,a(\nabla\times\mathbf{H}_h^{k+1/2},\mathbf{H}_h^{k+1/2},\mathbf{v}_h)
   -\langle P_h^{k+1},\nabla\cdot\mathbf{v}_h\rangle
   =\langle\mathbf{f}^{k+1/2},\mathbf{v}_h\rangle+\text{BCs},
   \end{aligned}
   \]
   together with the discrete vorticity definition and the strong divergence-free constraint. (The convective and Lorentz terms are evaluated at the mid-point, partially linearizing the system.)

2. **Maxwell step** (at half-integer times, using the newly obtained velocity):
   \[
   \Bigl\langle\frac{\mathbf{H}_h^{k+3/2}-\mathbf{H}_h^{k+1/2}}{\Delta t},\mathbf{b}_h\Bigr\rangle
   +R_m^{-1}\langle\nabla\times\mathbf{H}_h^{k+1},\nabla\times\mathbf{b}_h\rangle
   -a(\mathbf{u}_h^{k+1},\mathbf{H}_h^{k+1},\nabla\times\mathbf{b}_h)
   =\text{BCs}.
   \]

The scheme is second-order accurate in time, fully decouples the (nonlinear) fluid subsystem from the (linear) Maxwell subsystem, and retains the spatial structure-preserving properties. Energy conservation is sacrificed after decoupling, but the residual energy balance remains controlled and can be monitored by the hive’s existing energy-preserving filters.

**Conservation / dissipation theorems (paper)**  
- Strong discrete mass conservation: \(\nabla\cdot\mathbf{u}_h^{k}=0\) for all \(k\).  
- Strong discrete charge conservation.  
- Weak discrete Gauss law: \(\langle\nabla\cdot\mathbf{H}_h^{k},q_h\rangle=0\) (up to boundary contributions consistent with the continuous theory).  
- Discrete energy identity (coupled version) reproduces the exact viscous + ohmic dissipation; after decoupling the identity holds up to a controllable consistency error of order \(\mathcal{O}(\Delta t^2)\).

Optimal spatial convergence (matching the approximation order of the underlying finite-element spaces) and second-order temporal convergence are proved via manufactured solutions and confirmed on the classical Orszag–Tang vortex and lid-driven cavity MHD benchmarks.

### 3. Executable spectral / residual layer (NumPy / JAX skeleton)

```python
import jax
import jax.numpy as jnp
from jax import jit, vmap
from functools import partial

# --- Paper constants (non-dimensional) ---
Rf = 1000.0          # fluid Reynolds
Rm = 1000.0          # magnetic Reynolds
c  = 1.0             # coupling number
dt = 1e-3

# --- Discrete de-Rham projectors (hive already owns full Nédélec/RT; here spectral proxies) ---
@jit

def curl(vhat, k):
    """Spectral curl (k-space)."""
    return 1j * jnp.cross(k, vhat, axis=0)

@jit

def div(vhat, k):
    return 1j * jnp.sum(k * vhat, axis=0)

@jit

def project_divfree(vhat, k):
    """Qin-style H(div,0) projector (spectral)."""
    k2 = jnp.sum(k**2, axis=0) + 1e-30
    return vhat - k * (div(vhat, k) / k2)[None, ...]

# --- Skew-symmetric trilinear form a(α,β,γ) = ⟨α×β, γ⟩ ---
@jit

def trilinear(a, b, c):
    return jnp.sum(jnp.cross(a, b, axis=0) * c)

# --- Fluid residual (integer time step, Maxwell frozen) ---
@jit

def fluid_residual(u, omega, P, H, f, k):
    """Leapfrog fluid step residual (paper eq. after temporal discretization)."""
    conv = jnp.cross(omega, u, axis=0)
    visc = (1.0/Rf) * curl(omega, k)
    lorentz = -c * jnp.cross(curl(H, k), H, axis=0)
    gradP = 1j * k * P
    return (u - dt*(conv + visc + lorentz + gradP - f),
            omega - curl(u, k),
            div(u, k))           # strong mass conservation residual

# --- Maxwell residual (half-integer time step, velocity frozen) ---
@jit

def maxwell_residual(H, u, k):
    """Leapfrog Maxwell step residual."""
    induction = curl(jnp.cross(u, H, axis=0), k)
    diffusion = (1.0/Rm) * curl(curl(H, k), k)
    return H - dt*(induction - diffusion)

# --- Full staggered leapfrog residual monitor ---
@partial
(jit, static_argnums=(0,))
def decoupled_leapfrog_monitor(state, k, f, step):
    """
    state = (u, omega, P, H)
    Alternates fluid / Maxwell according to parity of step.
    Returns residual norms + structure-preservation diagnostics.
    """
    u, omega, P, H = state
    if step % 2 == 0:          # fluid step
        res_u, res_omega, res_div = fluid_residual(u, omega, P, H, f, k)
        res_H = jnp.zeros_like(H)
        stage = "fluid"
    else:                      # Maxwell step
        res_u = jnp.zeros_like(u)
        res_omega = jnp.zeros_like(omega)
        res_div = jnp.zeros_like(P)
        res_H = maxwell_residual(H, u, k)
        stage = "maxwell"

    mass_err   = jnp.linalg.norm(res_div)
    gauss_err  = jnp.linalg.norm(div(H, k))          # weak Gauss residual
    energy_res = 0.5*(jnp.sum(u**2) + c*jnp.sum(H**2))  # monitored energy
    return {
        "stage": stage,
        "mass_conservation_residual": mass_err,
        "gauss_law_residual": gauss_err,
        "energy_monitor": energy_res,
        "fluid_res_norm": jnp.linalg.norm(res_u),
        "maxwell_res_norm": jnp.linalg.norm(res_H),
        "scar_floor_protect": (mass_err < 1e-12) and (gauss_err < 1e-10)
    }
```
