#### 1. Jordan chain at an \(N\)th-order EP
Unperturbed non-Hermitian Hamiltonian \(H_0\) possesses a single right eigenvector \(|u_0\rangle\) and left eigenvector \(\langle v_0|\) at eigenvalue \(E_0\), completed by the Jordan chains
\[
(H_0-E_0)|u_m\rangle=|u_{m-1}\rangle,\qquad m=1,\dots,N-1,
\]
\[
\langle v_m|(H_0-E_0)=\langle v_{m-1}|,\qquad m=1,\dots,N-1,
\]
with biorthogonality
\[
\langle v_i|u_j\rangle=\delta_{i+j,N-1}.
\]
In the Jordan basis the unperturbed block is the pure Jordan matrix
\[
\mathcal{J}_N=\begin{pmatrix}
0&1&0&\cdots&0\\
0&0&1&\cdots&0\\
\vdots&\vdots&\vdots&\ddots&\vdots\\
0&0&0&\cdots&1\\
0&0&0&\cdots&0
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

#### 2. Perturbed eigenproblem and coefficient equations
Under perturbation \(H=H_0+\epsilon H_1\) the right eigenvector expands as
\[
|u\rangle=|u_0\rangle+\sum_{i=1}^{N-1}s_i|u_i\rangle.
\]
Left-multiplication by the left chain \(\langle v_k|\) yields the exact linear system
\[
\bigl(\mathcal{J}_N+\epsilon\mathcal{H}_1\bigr)S=\lambda S,
\]
where \(S=(1,s_1,\dots,s_{N-1})^\top\) and the transformed perturbation matrix has entries
\[
H_1^{p,q}=\langle v_p|H_1|u_q\rangle.
\]
(The matrix \(\mathcal{H}_1\) is indexed so that the top-left entry is \(H_1^{N-1,0}\).)

#### 3. Analytic determination of the spectral splitting exponent \(\alpha\)
The eigenvalue splitting satisfies \(\lambda\sim\epsilon^\alpha\). The value of \(\alpha\) is fixed by the *support* of \(\mathcal{H}_1\):

- **Case A (upper-triangular / “silent” support)**: all \(H_1^{p,q}=0\) whenever \(p+q<N\).  
  Characteristic polynomial \(p(\lambda,\epsilon,N)=(-\lambda)^N\).  
  \(\Rightarrow\lambda=0\) (no splitting).  

- **Case B (single nonzero entry \(H_1^{p,q}\) with \(p+q<N\))**:  
  Reduced equation \(\lambda^{N-p}-\epsilon H_1^{p,q}\lambda^q=0\).  
  Non-trivial roots
  \[
  \lambda_m=\epsilon^{1/(N-p-q)}\,(H_1^{p,q})^{1/(N-p-q)}\,e^{2im\pi/(N-p-q)},\qquad m=1,\dots,N-p-q.
  \]
  \(\Rightarrow\alpha=1/(N-p-q)\).  
  Range: \(1/N\le\alpha\le1\) according as \(0\le p+q\le N-1\).

- **Case C (constant-sum line \(p+q=j\))**:  
  - \(j\ge N\): no splitting.  
  - \(j=N-1\) (anti-diagonal): \(\lambda_m=\epsilon H_1^{N-m,m-1}\) \(\Rightarrow\alpha=1\).  
  - \(j\le(N-1)/2\):  
    \[
    \lambda_m=\epsilon^{1/(N-j)}\Biggl(\sum_{k=0}^j H_1^{j-k,k}\Biggr)^{1/(N-j)}\,e^{2im\pi/(N-j)}.
    \]
    \(\Rightarrow\alpha=1/(N-j)\).  
  - Intermediate \(j\) require higher-order analysis but are dominated, in the weak-\(\epsilon\) limit, by the *minimal* \(j\).

**Conjecture (Wang–Yan)**: when several distinct sums \(j\) are present, the *minimal* \(j_{\min}\) governs the leading exponent \(\alpha=1/(N-j_{\min})\) for sufficiently weak perturbations.
