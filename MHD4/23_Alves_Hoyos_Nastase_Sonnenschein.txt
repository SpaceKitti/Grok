### 1. Electromagnetic Hopfions – Bateman construction (null sector)

Source-free Maxwell in complex form (c = 1):
\[
\vec{F}=\vec{E}+i\vec{B},\qquad
\nabla\cdot\vec{F}=0,\qquad
\nabla\times\vec{F}=i\partial_t\vec{F}.
\]
Bateman ansatz:
\[
\vec{F}=\nabla\alpha\times\nabla\beta,\qquad\alpha,\beta\in\mathbb{C}.
\]
Null condition \(\vec{F}^2=0\) automatically enforces
\[
\vec{E}\cdot\vec{B}=0,\qquad\vec{E}^2-\vec{B}^2=0.
\]
Canonical Hopfion (Hopf index 1):
\[
\begin{aligned}
\alpha&=\frac{r^2-t^2-1+2iz}{r^2-t^2+1+2it},\\
\beta&=\frac{2(x-iy)}{r^2-t^2+1+2it},
\end{aligned}
\qquad r^2=x^2+y^2+z^2.
\]
Higher torus knots: \(\alpha\to\alpha^m\), \(\beta\to\beta^n\) yield linking number \(mn\).

Helicities
\[
\mathcal{H}_{ab}=\int\vec{A}_a\cdot\vec{B}_b\,d^3x
\]
satisfy \(\mathcal{H}_{em}=\mathcal{H}_{me}=0\) while \(\mathcal{H}_{ee}=\mathcal{H}_{mm}\neq0\) (amplitude-dependent). The map \(S^3\to S^3\) realises the Hopf fibration; the magnetic helicity equals the Hopf invariant
\[
\mathcal{H}(\phi)=\int_{S^3}\mathcal{A}\wedge\mathcal{F}=1.
\]

### 2. Rañada construction (non-null sector)

Complex scalar maps \(\phi,\theta:S^3\to S^2\):
\[
\begin{aligned}
F_{\mu\nu}&=\frac{\sqrt{a}}{2\pi i}\frac{\partial_\mu\bar\phi\partial_\nu\phi-\partial_\nu\bar\phi\partial_\mu\phi}{(1+|\phi|^2)^2},\\
{}^*F_{\mu\nu}&=\frac{\sqrt{a}}{2\pi i}\frac{\partial_\mu\bar\theta\partial_\nu\theta-\partial_\nu\bar\theta\partial_\mu\theta}{(1+|\theta|^2)^2}.
\end{aligned}
\]
Clebsch variables
\[
p=\frac{1}{1+|\phi|^2},\qquad q=\frac{\arg\phi}{2\pi}

\[
\vec{B}=\nabla p\times\nabla q,\qquad\vec{A}=p\nabla q+\nabla\chi.

\[
