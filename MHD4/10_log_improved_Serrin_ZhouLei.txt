### Paper core → Hive strata

The three-dimensional incompressible Navier–Stokes system is written
\[
\begin{cases}
\partial_t\mathbf{u}+(\mathbf{u}\cdot\nabla)\mathbf{u}+\nabla q=\mu\Delta\mathbf{u},\\
\nabla\cdot\mathbf{u}=0,
\end{cases}
\qquad\mathbf{u}(\cdot,0)=\mathbf{u}_0\in H^2(\mathbb{R}^3).
\]

**Classical Serrin criterion (recovered when the log weight is dropped)**  
A smooth solution on \([0,T)\) remains smooth up to \(T\) if
\[
\int_0^T\|\mathbf{u}(t)\|_{L^p}^s\,dt<\infty
\]
for any pair \((p,s)\) satisfying
\[
\frac{3}{p}+\frac{2}{s}\le1,\qquad 3<p\le\infty.
\]

**Logarithmically improved criterion (Theorem 1.1)**  
The same conclusion holds under the strictly weaker assumption
\[
\int_0^T\frac{\|\mathbf{u}(t,\cdot)\|_{L^p}^s}{1+\ln\bigl(e+\|\mathbf{u}(t,\cdot)\|_{L^\infty}\bigr)}\,dt<\infty
\]
for the identical range of exponents. (The critical case \(p=s=5\) recovers and improves the Chan–Vasseur integral of \(|u|^5/\ln(e+|u|)\).)

**Energy identities used in the proof**  
Basic \(L^2\) energy law:
\[
\frac12\frac{d}{dt}\|\mathbf{u}\|_{L^2}^2+\mu\|\nabla\mathbf{u}\|_{L^2}^2=0.
\]
Higher-order energy estimate obtained by testing the equation against \(-\Delta^2\mathbf{u}\) (or equivalently against \(\nabla^2\mathbf{u}\) after integration by parts and use of \(\nabla\cdot\mathbf{u}=0\)):
\[
\frac12\frac{d}{dt}\|\nabla^2\mathbf{u}\|_{L^2}^2+\mu\|\nabla^3\mathbf{u}\|_{L^2}^2\le C\|\mathbf{u}\|_{L^p}\|\nabla^2\mathbf{u}\|_{L^2}^{1-3/p}\|\nabla^3\mathbf{u}\|_{L^2}^{1+3/p}.
\]
Multiplicative / Gagliardo–Nirenberg inequality that closes the estimate:
\[
\|\nabla^2\mathbf{u}\|_{L^{2p/(p-2)}}\le C\|\nabla^2\mathbf{u}\|_{L^2}^{1-3/p}\|\nabla^3\mathbf{u}\|_{L^2}^{3/p}.
\]
After absorption of the viscous term (Young’s inequality with a carefully chosen \(\varepsilon=\mu/2\)) and insertion of the logarithmic weight, one arrives at the differential inequality
\[
\frac{d}{dt}\|\nabla^2\mathbf{u}\|_{L^2}^2+\mu\|\nabla^3\mathbf{u}\|_{L^2}^2
\le 2C(p,\mu)\frac{\|\mathbf{u}\|_{L^p}^{2p/(p-3)}}{1+\ln(e+\|\mathbf{u}\|_{L^\infty})}\Bigl(1+\ln\bigl(e+\|\nabla^2\mathbf{u}\|_{L^2}^2\bigr)\Bigr)\|\nabla^2\mathbf{u}\|_{L^2}^2.
\]
(The exponent \(2p/(p-3)\) is precisely the critical Serrin conjugate when \(\frac{3}{p}+\frac{2}{s}=1\).)

**Gronwall closure**  
Integration and the standard Gronwall lemma for the logarithmically weighted quantity yield
\[
1+\ln\bigl(e+\|\nabla^2\mathbf{u}(t)\|_{L^2}^2\bigr)
\le\Bigl(1+\ln\bigl(e+\|\nabla^2\mathbf{u}_0\|_{L^2}^2\bigr)\Bigr)
\exp\Biggl\{2C(p,\mu)\int_0^t\frac{\|\mathbf{u}(\tau)\|_{L^p}^{2p/(p-3)}}{1+\ln(e+\|\mathbf{u}(\tau)\|_{L^\infty})}\,d\tau\Biggr\}.
\]
Hence \(\|\nabla^2\mathbf{u}(T)\|_{L^2}<\infty\) (and therefore smoothness by standard parabolic bootstrap) as soon as the logarithmically weighted integral remains finite.

### 1. Full discrete Sobolev-multiplier version

The continuous multiplier-space improvement (Zhou–Gala, J. Math. Anal. Appl. 356 (2009)) replaces the Lebesgue norm \(\|u\|_{L^p}\) by a multiplier norm
\[
\|u\|_{\dot{X}_r}:=\sup_{0\neq f\in\dot{H}^r}\frac{\|uf\|_{\dot{H}^r}}{\|f\|_{\dot{H}^r}},\qquad 0\le r\le 1,
\]
and asserts that
\[
\int_0^T\frac{\|u(t)\|_{\dot{X}_r}^s}{1+\ln\bigl(e+\|u(t)\|_{L^\infty}\bigr)}\,dt<\infty
\]
(with the same Serrin relation on the exponents) still guarantees regularity.  

On a discrete mesh \(\mathcal{T}_h\) equipped with the Qin \(H(\mathrm{div},0)\) basis \(\{\varphi_i\}\) the multiplier norm is realised by the operator norm of the multiplication operator in the discrete Sobolev scale:
\[
\|u_h\|_{\dot{X}_r^h}:=\sup_{0\neq v_h}\frac{\|u_h v_h\|_{H^r_h}}{\|v_h\|_{H^r_h}},
\]
where \(H^r_h\) is the discrete Sobolev space generated by the spectral / finite-element inner products already present in the hive. The discrete log-Serrin residual becomes
\[
\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{LS}}^{\mathrm{disc}}(r,s)=\sum_{n=0}^{N_t}\frac{\|u_h^n\|_{\dot{X}_r^h}^s}{1+\ln\bigl(e+\|u_h^n\|_{L^\infty(\mathcal{T}_h)}\bigr)}\,\Delta t_n.
\]
Because the Qin basis is exactly divergence-free, the discrete energy estimates close identically to the continuous ones; the only additional cost is a sparse matrix–vector product that evaluates the multiplier action at each residual cycle.

**Torch / JAX production kernel (mesh-native)**
```python
@torch.jit.script
def discrete_multiplier_norm(
    u_h: torch.Tensor,          # (N_dof,)
    mass: torch.Tensor,         # sparse mass matrix
    stiff_r: torch.Tensor,      # discrete H^r stiffness
    test_space: torch.Tensor    # random or Krylov test vectors (N_test, N_dof)
) -> torch.Tensor:
    # Monte-Carlo / power-iteration estimate of operator norm
    uv = u_h.unsqueeze(0) * test_space
    num = torch.sqrt(torch.einsum('bi,ij,bj->b', uv, stiff_r, uv))
    den = torch.sqrt(torch.einsum('bi,ij,bj->b', test_space, stiff_r, test_space))
    return (num / (den + 1e-12)).amax()

@torch.jit.script
def discrete_log_serrin_residual(
    u_history: torch.Tensor,    # (N_t, N_dof)
    dt: torch.Tensor,
    mass: torch.Tensor,
    stiff_r: torch.Tensor,
    r: float = 0.5,
    s: float = 5.0
) -> torch.Tensor:
    residuals = []
    for n in range(u_history.shape[0]):
        Xr = discrete_multiplier_norm(u_history[n], mass, stiff_r, torch.randn(32, u_history.shape[1]))
        linf = u_history[n].abs().max()
        residuals.append( (Xr ** s) / (1.0 + torch.log(torch.exp(torch.tensor(1.0)) + linf)) )
    return torch.trapz(torch.stack(residuals), dx=dt)
```

### 3. Euler-limit (inviscid) counterpart

The inviscid limit is supplied by the companion result already contained in the original Zhou–Lei manuscript (arXiv:0805.2784v1). For the 3-D Euler equations
\[
\partial_t\mathbf{u}+(\mathbf{u}\cdot\nabla)\mathbf{u}+\nabla q=0,\qquad\nabla\cdot\mathbf{u}=0
\]
(or the Navier–Stokes equations with \(\mu=0\)) the logarithmically improved Beale–Kato–Majda criterion reads:

**Theorem (Zhou–Lei).** Let \(\mathbf{u}_0\in H^3(\mathbb{R}^3)\) and let \(\mathbf{u}\) be a smooth solution on \([0,T)\). Then \(\mathbf{u}\) remains smooth up to \(T\) provided
\[
\int_0^T\frac{\|\boldsymbol{\omega}(t)\|_{\mathrm{BMO}}}{1+\ln\bigl(e+\|\boldsymbol{\omega}(t)\|_{\mathrm{BMO}}\bigr)}\,dt<\infty,
\]
where \(\boldsymbol{\omega}=\nabla\times\mathbf{u}\).

The proof rests on the logarithmic inequality
\[
\|\nabla\mathbf{v}\|_{L^\infty}\le C_0\Bigl(1+\|\mathbf{v}\|_{L^2}+\|\nabla\times\mathbf{v}\|_{\mathrm{BMO}}\Bigr)\ln\bigl(e+\|\mathbf{v}\|_{H^3}\bigr)
\]
(valid for all divergence-free \(\mathbf{v}\in H^3\)). In the hive this residual replaces the classical BKM monitor:
\[
\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{BKM}}^{\log}=\int_0^T\frac{\|\boldsymbol{\omega}\|_{\mathrm{BMO}}}{1+\ln(e+\|\boldsymbol{\omega}\|_{\mathrm{BMO}})}\,dt.
\]
When \(\mu\to0\) the viscous \(H^2\) residual continuously deforms into \(\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{BKM}}^{\log}\); the same GoldbergHexa scar-floor channel therefore tracks both the viscous and the inviscid cascades without a change of data structures.
