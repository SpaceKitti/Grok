Continuum acoustics with designed loss is the Helmholtz / first-order system

\]
Equivalently the non-Hermitian Helmholtz pencil
\[

\[

- particle velocity \(\mathbf{v}_h\in H(\mathrm{div})_h\simeq V_h^2\) (Qin / RT family, contravariant Piola),

The discrete complex mass and stiffness are
\[

A[p,q]=\int_{\mathcal{T}_h}\rho^{-1}\nabla_m p\cdot\nabla_m q\,dV.

def skin_weighted_volterra(
    lambda_shear: torch.Tensor,
    W_boundary: torch.Tensor,
    s: int,
    dv: float,
    alpha: float = 1.0
) -> torch.Tensor:
    cum = torch.cumsum(lambda_shear * dv, dim=0)
    delta = cum.unsqueeze(1) - cum.unsqueeze(0)
