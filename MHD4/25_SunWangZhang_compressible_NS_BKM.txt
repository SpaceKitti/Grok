### Paper core → Hive strata

The isentropic compressible Navier–Stokes system on \(\Omega=\mathbb{R}^3\) or a bounded domain is
\[
\partial_t\rho+\operatorname{div}(\rho u)=0,
\]
\[
\partial_t(\rho u)+\operatorname{div}(\rho u\otimes u)-Lu+\nabla p=0,
\]
with \(p=a\rho^\gamma\) (\(\gamma>1\)), Lamé operator
\[
Lu=\mu\Delta u+(\mu+\lambda)\nabla\operatorname{div}u,\qquad\mu>0,\quad 3\lambda+2\mu\ge0.
\]
Initial vacuum \(\rho_0\ge0\) is permitted. Local strong solutions exist; \(T^*\) denotes the maximal existence time.

**Theorem 1.3 (Sun–Wang–Zhang).** Assume \(\lambda<7\mu\). If \(T^*<\infty\), then
\[
\limsup_{T\uparrow T^*}\|\rho\|_{L^\infty(0,T;L^\infty(\Omega))}=\infty.
\]
In other words, a strong solution remains regular on \([0,T]\) as soon as the density stays uniformly bounded from above. This is the precise compressible analogue of the classical Beale–Kato–Majda vorticity criterion.

**Effective viscous flux.** Introduce the auxiliary field \(v=L^{-1}\nabla p(\rho)\) solving the Lamé system
\[
\mu\Delta v+(\lambda+\mu)\nabla\operatorname{div}v=\nabla p(\rho),\qquad v|_{\partial\Omega}=0.
\]
Set \(w=u-v\). Then the effective viscous flux
\[
G:=(\lambda+2\mu)\operatorname{div}w=(\lambda+2\mu)\operatorname{div}u-p
\]
satisfies a linear parabolic equation whose coefficients remain controlled once \(\|\rho\|_{L^\infty}\le M<\infty\). Under that bound one obtains the high-integrability estimate
\[
\rho|u|^r\in L^\infty(0,T;L^1(\Omega))\qquad(r\in(3,6))
\]
and the regularity
\[
\|\nabla w\|_{L^\infty(0,T;L^2)}+\|\rho^{1/2}\partial_tw\|_{L^2}+\|\nabla^2w\|_{L^2((0,T)\times\Omega)}\le C(M).
\]
Consequently
\[
\|\nabla u\|_{L^\infty(0,T;L^2)}+\|u\|_{L^\infty(0,T;L^6)}+\|\nabla u\|_{L^2(0,T;L^q)}\le C,\qquad q\in[2,6].
\]
The equation for \(\nabla\rho\),
\[
\partial_t\nabla\rho+(u\cdot\nabla)\nabla\rho+\nabla u\cdot\nabla\rho+\operatorname{div}u\,\nabla\rho+\rho\nabla\operatorname{div}u=0,
\]
then yields, after multiplication by \(q|\nabla\rho|^{q-2}\nabla\rho\) and Gronwall, a uniform bound on \(\|\nabla\rho\|_{L^q}\). This contradicts the known blow-up criterion of Cho–Choe–Kim and closes the argument.

@torch.jit.script
def discrete_lame_reconstruct(
    p_h: torch.Tensor,          # (n_elem, n_nodes)
    mass: torch.Tensor,         # Qin mass matrix
    stiff_mu: torch.Tensor,     # discrete μ ∇_m · ∇_m
    stiff_lam: torch.Tensor,    # discrete (λ+μ) div_m · div_m
    lam: float, mu: float
) -> torch.Tensor:
    # assemble discrete Lamé operator
    A = mu * stiff_mu + (lam + mu) * stiff_lam
