### Key mathematical apparatus (hive-ready, all symbols dimensionless as in §4)

**Geometry & fields**  
\[
\mathbf{u}=U_0(0,u(x),0),\qquad\mathbf{B}=B_0(1,b(x),0),\qquad x\in[-1/2,1/2].
\]
No-slip + continuous tangential \(\mathbf{B}\): \(u=\pm1/2=0\), \(b(\pm1/2)=0\).

**Strain-rate & unit vector**  
\[
W_{xy}=W_{yx}=U_0\frac{du}{dx},\qquad\hat{\mathbf{b}}=\frac{(1,b,0)}{\sqrt{1+b^2}}.
\]

**Total \(xy\)-stress (exact Braginskii reduction)**  
\[
\sigma_{xy}=\Bigl[\mu_\perp+(\mu_\parallel-\mu_\perp)\frac{2b^2}{(1+b^2)^2}\Bigr]U_0\frac{du}{dx}.
\]
(Equivalent phenomenological regularisation \(\boldsymbol{\sigma}=(\mu_\parallel-\mu_\perp)\hat{\mathbf{b}}\hat{\mathbf{b}}:\mathbf{W}+\mu_\perp\mathbf{W}\).)

**Dimensionless ODE after elimination of \(u\) (integrated once, odd symmetry)**  
\[
0=fx+b-\frac1{Ha^2}\Biggl(\varepsilon^2+(1-\varepsilon^2)\frac{2b^2}{(1+b^2)^2}\Biggr)\frac{d^2b}{dx^2},
\]

\[
Ha=\frac{B_0L}{(4\pi\lambda\mu_\parallel)^{1/2}},\qquad f=\frac{4\pi FL}{B_0^2},\qquad\varepsilon=\sqrt{\frac{\mu_\perp}{\mu_\parallel}}.
\]

**Velocity reconstruction**  
\[
u(x)=\Bigl(\frac{db}{dx}\Bigr)_{\mathrm{wall}}-\frac{db}{dx},\qquad u_{\max}=\Bigl(\frac{db}{dx}\Bigr)_{\mathrm{wall}}+f.

**Core equation (\(\varepsilon=0\), \(b\gg\varepsilon\))**  
\[
0=fx+b-\frac1{Ha^2}\frac{2b^2}{(1+b^2)^2}\frac{d^2b}{dx^2}.

Exact solution \(b=-fx\); linearised regular singular point at \(x=0\) yields  
\[
\tilde{b}\sim x^{r_+},\qquad r_+=\tfrac12+\sqrt{\delta^2+\tfrac14},\qquad\delta=\frac{Ha}{\sqrt{2f}}.

\[
b\sim\tfrac12\,3^{2/3}f^{1/3}Ha^{2/3}\Bigl(x+\tfrac12\Bigr)^{2/3}\quad(x\to-1/2).

**Hartmann layers (\(Ha\gg1\))**  
Rescale \(x=-1/2+Y/Ha\). Leading-order balance Maxwell vs parallel viscosity:  
\[
\frac{d^2b}{dY^2}=-V'(b),\qquad V(b)=\bigl(b+\tfrac16b^3-\tfrac1{2b}\bigr)f-b^2-\log b-\tfrac14b^4.
\]
Energy integral  
\[
\Bigl(\frac{db}{dY}\Bigr)^2=\mathcal{E}-V(b),\qquad\mathcal{E}=V(f/2).
\]
Implicit solution  
\[
Y=\int_0^b\bigl(\mathcal{E}-V(s)\bigr)^{-1/2}\,ds.
\]
Asymptotics: \(b\sim\tfrac12\,3^{2/3}f^{1/3}Y^{2/3}\) (\(Y\to0\)), exponential decay to core (\(Y\to\infty\)). Infinite wall current.
