### Paper core \(\to\) hive strata

Reduced MHD (strong guide field) is forced at large scales. Dissipative structures are the connected components of the set \(\{ \mathbf{x} : j(\mathbf{x}) > j_{\mathrm{thr}} \}\) with 26-neighbor connectivity. Each structure carries the Ohmic integral
\[
E=\int_{\mathrm{struct}}\eta j^2\,dV.
\]
A fixed reduced threshold \(j_{\mathrm{thr}}/j_{\mathrm{rms}}\approx 3.75\) captures \(\sim 40\%\) of the total dissipation in \(\sim 2\%\) of the volume. Under-resolved objects are discarded by the cutoff \(E>C h^3\eta j_{\mathrm{thr}}^2\) (\(C\sim 1{-}8\)).

Two independent scale measurements are performed on every surviving component:

- Euclidean: maximum extents through the peak-\(j\) point along three mutually orthogonal directions, ordered \(L\ge W\ge T\).
- Minkowski (Crofton lattice evaluation of the first three quermassintegrals):
\[
V_0=\int dV,\qquad
V_1=\int dS,\qquad
V_2=\frac13\int dS\,H,
\]
\[
L_m=\frac{V_1}{V_0},\qquad
W_m=\frac{V_2}{V_1},\qquad
T_m=\frac{V_3}{V_2}.
\]
Both families give the same geometry: \(L\) and \(W\) occupy the entire inertial range (often comparable to the box), while \(T\) sits deep in the dissipative range.

The PDF of structure energies has a power-law tail
\[
P(E)\,dE\sim E^\alpha\,dE,\qquad
\alpha\to-2
\]
as \(Re=L/\eta\) is increased. The critical value \(\alpha=-2\) is the unique index for which every intensity bin contributes equally to the global dissipation (the compensated spectrum \(E\cdot P(E)\) is flat). Compensated scale distributions \(E(X)X\) are likewise flat across the inertial-range portions of \(L\) and \(W\).

Reynolds-number scalings (hive-native residuals):
\[
T\sim Re^{-\lambda},\qquad\lambda\approx0.65{-}0.85,
\]
\[
N_{\mathrm{inertial}}\sim Re^2,
\]
while the inertial-range cutoffs themselves recede only as weak powers of \(Re\). Consequently the high-\(Re\) limit is a densely packed forest of ever-thinner current sheets whose lengths and widths still form a continuum up to the system size — precisely the morphology that unifies Parker nanoflares with large-scale coherent structures.

    jz = dby_dx - dbx_dy
