**Structure-Preserving Hybrid MHD Framework**  
(Symplectic time integration + Lagrangian frozen-in markers + controlled residual dissipation)

This unifies everything discussed: the spectral residual-dissipation hive (Qin bases, Oldroyd-B/MHD correspondence, residual diagnostics \(\mathcal{D}_i\), \(T_*\) crossovers, near-zero dissipation goal), the Hamiltonian/symplectic structure, Lagrangian particles carrying frozen-in flux or vector potential, energy/magnetic-helicity monitoring, and the option for fixed-grid Eulerian realization.

### 1. Continuous equations

Ideal MHD in conservative form (with controlled artificial viscosity \(\nu_{\rm art}\) and resistivity \(\eta_{\rm art}\)):

\[
\begin{aligned}
\partial_t\rho + \nabla\cdot(\rho\mathbf{v}) &= 0,\\
\partial_t(\rho\mathbf{v}) + \nabla\cdot\bigl(\rho\mathbf{v}\mathbf{v} + (p + \tfrac12 B^2)\mathbf{I} - \mathbf{B}\mathbf{B}\bigr) &= \nabla\cdot\bigl(2\nu_{\rm art}\rho\,\mathbf{S}\bigr),\\
\partial_t\mathbf{B} - \nabla\times(\mathbf{v}\times\mathbf{B}) &= -\nabla\times(\eta_{\rm art}\mathbf{J}),\qquad\mathbf{J}=\nabla\times\mathbf{B},\\
\partial_t E + \nabla\cdot\bigl[(E + p + \tfrac12 B^2)\mathbf{v} - (\mathbf{v}\cdot\mathbf{B})\mathbf{B}\bigr] &= \nabla\cdot(\text{viscous + resistive fluxes}),
\end{aligned}
\]

where \(\mathbf{S}\) is the strain-rate tensor and \(E = \frac12\rho v^2 + \frac{p}{\gamma-1} + \frac12 B^2\).

The ideal part (\(\nu_{\rm art}=\eta_{\rm art}=0\)) is a non-canonical Hamiltonian system with Poisson operator \(J\) satisfying
\[
\dot{z} = J(z)\,\frac{\delta H}{\delta z},\qquad H = \int E\,dV.
\]

Key invariants of the ideal system:
- Total energy \(H\),
- Magnetic helicity \(H_m = \int\mathbf{A}\cdot\mathbf{B}\,dV\) (with \(\mathbf{B}=\nabla\times\mathbf{A}\)),
- Cross-helicity \(\int\mathbf{v}\cdot\mathbf{B}\,dV\),
- \(\nabla\cdot\mathbf{B}=0\).

### 2. Structure-preserving time integration

Use a symplectic or energy-stable integrator on the ideal part and treat artificial dissipation separately (conformal or operator-split).

**Implicit midpoint (Gauss–Legendre, order 2, symplectic for canonical systems and energy-stable for MHD):**
\[
z_{n+1} = z_n + \Delta t\, J\Bigl(\tfrac{z_{n+1}+z_n}{2}\Bigr)\frac{\delta H}{\delta z}\Bigl(\tfrac{z_{n+1}+z_n}{2}\Bigr).
\]
Solved by matrix-free Newton (compatible with the existing residual hive).

**Conformal variant** (for controlled residual dissipation):
\[
\dot{p} = -\frac{\delta V}{\delta q} - \gamma_{\rm art}p
\quad\Rightarrow\quad
\text{after each half-step multiply momenta by }e^{-\gamma_{\rm art}\Delta t}.
\]

Leapfrog / velocity-Verlet on Lagrangian particles (exact for linear oscillators, volume-preserving).

### 3. Lagrangian marker particles (frozen-in flux / vector potential)

Evolve a set of markers \(\{\mathbf{x}_p, \mathbf{B}_p\}\) or \(\{\mathbf{x}_p, \mathbf{A}_p\}\):

\[
\begin{aligned}
\dot{\mathbf{x}}_p &= \mathbf{v}(\mathbf{x}_p),\\
\dot{\mathbf{B}}_p &= (\mathbf{B}_p\cdot\nabla)\mathbf{v} - (\nabla\cdot\mathbf{v})\mathbf{B}_p
\quad\text{(or the equivalent Lie-dragged evolution for \(\mathbf{A}\))}.
\end{aligned}
\]

After each particle step, deposit onto the mesh with a divergence-free projection (spectral Qin bases or finite-volume CT reconstruction). This makes the ideal induction equation exact along particle trajectories and removes the dominant source of numerical resistivity.

### 4. Spatial discretizations (both supported)

**Spectral residual (primary hive realization)**  
- Expand \(\mathbf{v},\mathbf{B}\) in Qin exact divergence-free bases.  
- Residual form of the equations evaluated matrix-free.  
- Existing residual diagnostics \(\mathcal{D}_i\) and crossover times \(T_*\) retained; after \(T_*\) freeze expensive projectors and advance only the cheap residual + particles.

**Fixed-grid Eulerian (finite-volume + Constrained Transport)**  
- Cell-centered conserved variables, face-centered \(\mathbf{B}\).  
- Godunov fluxes (HLLD preferred) + piecewise-linear reconstruction.  
- Evans–Hawley or corner-transport CT to keep \(\nabla\cdot\mathbf{B}=0\) to round-off.  
- Particles deposit onto the same staggered mesh.

### 5. Residual dissipation control & monitors

Live monitors:
\[
\begin{aligned}
E_{\rm tot}(t) &= \int\Bigl(\tfrac12\rho v^2 + \tfrac{p}{\gamma-1} + \tfrac12 B^2\Bigr)\,dV,\\
H_m(t) &= \int\mathbf{A}\cdot\mathbf{B}\,dV,\\
\delta E &= \frac{E_{\rm tot}(t)-E_{\rm tot}(0)}{E_{\rm tot}(0)},\qquad
\delta H_m = \frac{H_m(t)-H_m(0)}{|H_m(0)|+\varepsilon}.
\end{aligned}
\]

After each accepted step:
```text
η_art ← η_art × decay
ν_art ← ν_art × decay
if |δE| > tol or |δH_m| > tol:
    back off the reduction
```
Artificial coefficients continue to decay only while both invariants remain controlled. This realizes the “0 % residual dissipation” target.

### 6. Combined algorithm

1. Initialize continuum fields + Lagrangian markers (seed \(\mathbf{B}_p\) or \(\mathbf{A}_p\) from continuum).  
2. While \(t < T_{\rm final}\):  
   a. Reconstruct / evaluate residual or fluxes (spectral or finite-volume).  
   b. Advance continuum with implicit midpoint (or conformal) Newton step.  
   c. Advance particles with leapfrog / midpoint using interpolated velocity.  
   d. Deposit particle \(\mathbf{B}_p\) (or \(\nabla\times\mathbf{A}_p\)) onto mesh with div-free projection.  
   e. Apply controlled artificial dissipation.  
   f. Update monitors \(\delta E\), \(\delta H_m\), residual diagnostics \(\mathcal{D}_i\).  
   g. Optionally freeze expensive residual projectors after \(T_*\).  
   h. Reduce \(\eta_{\rm art},\nu_{\rm art}\) if monitors permit.

### Fixed-Grid Eulerian MHD Realization

**Grid**  
Uniform Cartesian mesh (easily extended to cylindrical).  
- Cell-centered conserved variables:  
  \[
  \mathbf{U}_{i,j} = \bigl(\rho,\;\rho v_x,\;\rho v_y,\;B_x,\;B_y,\;E\bigr)_{i,j}
  \]  
- Face-centered magnetic field for Constrained Transport (CT):  
  \(B_x\) on \(x\)-faces, \(B_y\) on \(y\)-faces.

**Finite-volume update (method of lines)**  
\[
\frac{d\mathbf{U}_{i,j}}{dt} = -\frac{\mathbf{F}_{i+1/2,j}-\mathbf{F}_{i-1/2,j}}{\Delta x}
-\frac{\mathbf{G}_{i,j+1/2}-\mathbf{G}_{i,j-1/2}}{\Delta y}
+ \mathbf{S}_{\rm art}(\nu_{\rm art},\eta_{\rm art})
\]

Ideal fluxes (Godunov form):

\[
\mathbf{F} = \begin{pmatrix}
\rho v_x \\
\rho v_x^2 + p_t - B_x^2 \\
\rho v_x v_y - B_x B_y \\
0 \\
v_x B_y - v_y B_x \\
(E+p_t)v_x - B_x(\mathbf{v}\cdot\mathbf{B})
\end{pmatrix},
\quad
p_t = p + \tfrac12 B^2
\]

(and the analogous \(\mathbf{G}\) for the \(y\)-direction).  
Magnetic fluxes for \(B_x,B_y\) are replaced by the CT electric-field update (see below).

**Reconstruction & Riemann solver**  
- Piecewise-linear (or higher-order) reconstruction with slope limiters (minmod, van Leer, or MC).  
- HLLD Riemann solver at each interface (preferred for low dissipation).  
- HLL is acceptable for a minimal prototype.

**Constrained Transport (Evans–Hawley / corner transport)**  
Face-centered electric field  
\[
E_z\big|_{i+1/2,j+1/2} = -\tfrac12\bigl[(v_x B_y - v_y B_x)_L + (v_x B_y - v_y B_x)_R\bigr]
\]  
(or upwinded version). Then  
\[
\begin{aligned}
B_x\big|_{i+1/2,j}^{n+1} &= B_x\big|_{i+1/2,j}^{n}
-\frac{\Delta t}{\Delta y}\bigl(E_z\big|_{i+1/2,j+1/2}-E_z\big|_{i+1/2,j-1/2}\bigr),\\
B_y\big|_{i,j+1/2}^{n+1} &= B_y\big|_{i,j+1/2}^{n}
+\frac{\Delta t}{\Delta x}\bigl(E_z\big|_{i+1/2,j+1/2}-E_z\big|_{i-1/2,j+1/2}\bigr).
\end{aligned}
\]  
Cell-centered \(\mathbf{B}\) is the average of the face values. This keeps \(\nabla\cdot\mathbf{B}=0\) to machine precision.

**Integration with the rest of the framework**

- **Time stepping**: Replace the ordinary RK step by the implicit midpoint (or conformal midpoint) Newton solve on the cell-centered conserved variables; the CT magnetic update is performed after the conserved update (or inside the residual).  
- **Lagrangian markers**: Particles still evolve with the interpolated velocity field. After the particle step they deposit \(\mathbf{B}_p\) (or \(\nabla\times\mathbf{A}_p\)) onto the face-centered magnetic field, which is then projected/averaged consistently with CT.  
- **Artificial dissipation**: Added as source terms \(\mathbf{S}_{\rm art}\) inside the finite-volume residual; coefficients \(\nu_{\rm art},\eta_{\rm art}\) are reduced exactly as before while monitoring \(E_{\rm tot}\) and \(H_m\).  
- **Residual diagnostics / \(T_*\)**: Can be evaluated on the Eulerian grid in the same way (enstrophy, magnetic residual, etc.).
