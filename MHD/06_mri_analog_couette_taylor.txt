### 1. Exact governing system (Oldroyd-B + solvent)

\[
\rho\Bigl(\partial_t\mathbf{u}+\mathbf{u}\cdot\nabla\mathbf{u}\Bigr)=-\nabla p+\nabla\cdot\mathbf{T}+\mu_s\nabla^2\mathbf{u},
\]
\[
\nabla\cdot\mathbf{u}=0,
\]
\[
\mathbf{T}+\tau\Bigl[\partial_t\mathbf{T}+\mathbf{u}\cdot\nabla\mathbf{T}-(\nabla\mathbf{u})^\top\cdot\mathbf{T}-\mathbf{T}\cdot\nabla\mathbf{u}\Bigr]=\mu_p\bigl(\nabla\mathbf{u}+(\nabla\mathbf{u})^\top\bigr).
\]

(The upper-convected derivative is identical to the ideal induction operator that freezes magnetic field lines; the solvent viscosity \(\mu_s\) supplies the residual Newtonian floor already monitored by \(\mathcal{E}_{\rm AN}\).)

### 2. Basic Couette–Taylor state (exact)

\[
\mathbf{u}=r\Omega(r)\,\mathbf{e}_\phi,\qquad\Omega(r)=A+Br^{-2},
\]
with \(A,B\) fixed by the cylinder angular velocities \(\Omega_{1,2}\) at radii \(r_{1,2}\). The only non-zero polymeric stresses are
\[
T_{r\phi}=-2\mu_p B r^{-2},\qquad T_{\phi\phi}=8\mu_p\tau B^2 r^{-4}.
\]
The hoop stress is the precise analog of a toroidal field amplitude \(B_\phi=(\mu_0 T_{\phi\phi})^{1/2}\). Dimensionless groups used by the hive residual:
\[
\mathrm{Re}=\frac{r_1 d|\Omega_2-\Omega_1|}{\nu},\qquad
\mathrm{De}=\frac{r_1|\Omega_2-\Omega_1|\tau}{d},\qquad
\nu=\frac{\mu_s+\mu_p}{\rho},\quad d=r_2-r_1.
\]

Three rotation profiles are hard-wired as hive switches:
- Case 1 (Keplerian, \(p=-3/2\)): \(\Omega_2/\Omega_1=(r_2/r_1)^{-3/2}\) — pure MRI-analog + EI, no CI.
- Case 2 (\(p=+3/2\)): only EI (suppressed by increasing Re).
- Case 3 (\(p=-3\)): coexistence of CI (m=0), EI and MRI-analog with continuous mode transitions.

### 3. Linear residual operator (sixth-order ODE system)

Perturbations of the form \(\exp(st+im\phi+ikz)\) convert the system into a sixth-order radial eigenvalue problem for the complex growth rate \(s\). No-slip conditions are imposed at \(r=r_1,r_2\). The three instability channels are distinguished by their energy sources and preferred wavenumbers:

- **Centrifugal (CI)** — large Re, De≃0; requires \(d(r^2\Omega)/dr<0\); prefers m=0 Taylor vortices.
- **Elastic (EI)** — large De, Re≃0; curvature-driven; critical De≳19 (case 1); prefers high k.
- **MRI-analog** — simultaneous large Re and De; requires \(d\Omega/dr<0\) (even when angular momentum increases outward); driven by shear energy via polymer “tethers”; preferred azimuthal mode number m increases with Re/De as the equivalent toroidal field weakens; vertical wavelength ∼ gap width.

At onset of the MRI-analog in case 1 (De≃3.3, Re≃40) the preferred mode is already non-axisymmetric (m=11, k≃95) and forms a rotating spiral. Maximum growth rates reach \(\mathrm{Re}(s)/\Omega_1\simeq0.35\) (case 1) and \(\simeq0.7\) (case 3). In the upper-right of the (De,Re) plane many modes are simultaneously unstable—exactly the turbulent regime the hive residual is designed to capture.

### 4. Hive residual diagnostics (live, on-device)

Three new algebraic monitors are braided into the existing diagnostic stream \(\{\mathcal{W},\mathcal{E},\mathcal{E}_{\rm AN},\alpha_o,\dots\}\):

\[
\begin{align*}
\alpha_{\rm MRI}(t)&=\frac{d}{d\log(1+t)}\log\|\mathbf{T}_{\phi\phi}(t)\|_{L^2},\\
\mathcal{D}_1^{\rm MRI}(t)&=\bigl|\alpha_{\rm MRI}(t)-\alpha_\omega(t)\bigr|,\\
\mathcal{D}_2^{\rm MRI}(t)&=\frac{\|\nabla\cdot\mathbf{T}\|_{L^2}}{\|\nu\Delta\mathbf{u}+\nabla\cdot\mathbf{T}\|_{L^2}+\delta},\\
\mathcal{D}_3^{\rm MRI}(t)&=\frac{\bigl|\langle\boldsymbol{\omega},\operatorname{curl}(\nabla\cdot\mathbf{T})\rangle\bigr|}{\|\boldsymbol{\omega}\|_{L^2}^2+\delta}.
\end{align*}
\]

The quantitative crossover time that freezes the expensive full Oldroyd-B projector after the MRI-analog residual has collapsed to a pure wave-like polymer tether is
\[
T_*^{\rm MRI}=\inf\bigl\{t>T_*^{B}:\;\mathcal{D}_1^{\rm MRI}<10^{-3},\;\mathcal{D}_2^{\rm MRI}<10^{-2},\;\mathcal{D}_3^{\rm MRI}<10^{-3}\bigr\}.
\]
