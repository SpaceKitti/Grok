### 1. Generalized Jacobi polynomials and derivative relations (paper §2.2)

The foundational 1-D building blocks are the generalized Jacobi polynomials of negative integer parameters:

\[
P_n^{(-1,-1)}(\xi)=\begin{cases}
\frac{1-\xi}{2},&n=0,\\
\frac{1+\xi}{2},&n=1,\\
\frac{\xi^2-1}{4}P_{n-2}^{(1,1)}(\xi),&n\geq2,
\end{cases}
\qquad
P_n^{(-2,-2)}(\xi)=\begin{cases}
\frac{(1-\xi)^2(2+\xi)}{4},&n=0,\\
\frac{(1-\xi)^2(1+\xi)}{4},&n=1,\\
\frac{(1+\xi)^2(2-\xi)}{4},&n=2,\\
\frac{(1+\xi)^2(\xi-1)}{4},&n=3,\\
\Bigl(\frac{\xi^2-1}{4}\Bigr)^2P_{n-4}^{(2,2)}(\xi),&n\geq4.
\end{cases}
\]

Orthogonality (with respect to the singular weights that enforce boundary conformity):

\[
\int_{-1}^{1}P_n^{(-1,-1)}(\xi)P_m^{(-1,-1)}(\xi)(1-\xi^2)^{-1}\,d\xi=\frac{n-1}{2n(2n-1)}\delta_{mn},\quad n,m\geq2,
\]

\[
\int_{-1}^{1}P_n^{(-2,-2)}(\xi)P_m^{(-2,-2)}(\xi)(1-\xi^2)^{-2}\,d\xi=\frac{(n-2)(n-3)}{8n(n-1)(2n-3)}\delta_{mn},\quad n,m\geq4.
\]

Critical derivative relations that guarantee exact cancellation of the divergence:

\[
\frac{d}{d\xi}P_n^{(-1,-1)}(\xi)=\frac{n-1}{2}P_{n-1}^{(0,0)}(\xi),\qquad
\frac{d}{d\xi}P_n^{(-2,-2)}(\xi)=\frac{n-3}{2}P_{n-1}^{(-1,-1)}(\xi).
\]

Normalized 1-D factors used throughout:

\[

### 3. Fully discrete DF-BDF-k schemes (paper §3)

\[

The \(k\)-step BDF operator is written

\[
\frac{\gamma\mathbf{v}^{n+1}-\hat{\mathbf{v}}^{n+1}}{\tau}=\partial_t\mathbf{v}\big|_{t_{n+1}}+O(\tau^k),

The fully discrete weak form (after integration by parts and use of the exact divergence-free property) reads, for all test functions \(\boldsymbol{\chi}\in\mathcal{H}_{N,0}^{1(\operatorname{div}0)}\), \(\boldsymbol{\Phi}\in\mathcal{H}_{N,0}^{\operatorname{div}0}\):

\[
\begin{aligned}
&\Bigl(\frac{\gamma\mathbf{u}^{n+1}-\hat{\mathbf{u}}^{n+1}}{\tau},\boldsymbol{\chi}\Bigr)
+\nu(\nabla\mathbf{u}^{n+1},\nabla\boldsymbol{\chi})
+(\mathbf{u}^{*,n+1}\cdot\nabla\mathbf{u}^{n+1},\boldsymbol{\chi})\\
&\qquad+(\mathbf{B}^{*,n+1}\times(\nabla\times\mathbf{B}^{n+1}),\boldsymbol{\chi})

&\Bigl(\frac{\gamma\mathbf{B}^{n+1}-\hat{\mathbf{B}}^{n+1}}{\tau},\boldsymbol{\Phi}\Bigr)
