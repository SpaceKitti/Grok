### 2. Time-dependent Krylov subspace and Lanczos coefficients (paper §III)

Under the \(t,t\) formalism the Krylov generators obey
\[
\hat{K}_{k+1}(t)=\hat{H}(t)\,i\partial_t\hat{K}_k(t)-a_k(t)\hat{K}_k(t)-b_k(t)\hat{K}_{k-1}(t),
\]
orthonormal with respect to the instantaneous inner product. The Lanczos matrix is the real symmetric tridiagonal
\[
\hat{L}(t)=\begin{pmatrix}
a_0(t)&b_1(t)&0&\cdots\\
b_1(t)&a_1(t)&b_2(t)&\cdots\\
0&b_2(t)&a_2(t)&\cdots\\
\vdots&\vdots&\vdots&\ddots
\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Krylov complexity is the expectation value of the position operator on the Krylov chain:
\[
C_K(t)=\langle\hat\phi(t)|\hat{K}|\hat\phi(t)\rangle=\sum_{k=0}^{d-1}k\,|\phi_k(t)|^2,
\]
where the amplitude vector evolves by the Schrödinger equation on the Lanczos matrix,
\[
i\partial_t\hat\phi(t)=\hat{L}(t)\hat\phi(t),\qquad\hat\phi(0)=\mathbf{e}_0.
\]

**Vanishing theorem.** If the initial Krylov seed is an instantaneous eigenstate \(|\hat{K}_0\rangle=|m(t)\rangle\), adiabatic evolution forces \(b_k\equiv0\) and \(C_K(t)\equiv0\).

**Non-vanishing for superpositions.** Choose
\[
|\hat{K}_0(t)\rangle=\cos\alpha(t)|m(t)\rangle+\sin\alpha(t)\,e^{i\beta(t)}|n(t)\rangle.
\]
The Lanczos coefficients become non-trivial; in particular \(b_1\neq0\) and the complexity is driven by the off-diagonal mixing.
